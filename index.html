Compliance — permits, roadworthiness & licensing

Cross-border checklist (Road Traffic Act & SADC corridors)

  • In-cab originals: vehicle licence & COF (roadworthy), load documents, customs bill of entry / manifest.
  • SADC cross-border: temporary import permit for the destination country, customs bond where applicable, third-party insurance valid in every country crossed.
  • Driver documents: Code EC licence, PDP/PrDP as applicable, passport and work permits where required, border checkpoint log.
  • Verify axle-load limits and gross combination mass per transit country before dispatch — overloads are the most common source of regional fines.
  • Apply the local rules of each transit country (Road Traffic & Transport Act in Namibia and equivalent legislation elsewhere); permits and COF are tracked below with alerts at 60 / 30 / 14 days.
